**Prithvi Raghu**. I study computer science at VIT Vellore and research quantum machine learning and scientific computing.

I build quantum convolutional networks, study multiplayer quantum games and compare circuit encodings for option pricing. I also model HF radio propagation and evaluate ionospheric corrections for emitter geolocation.

**Selected work**

* [QHD](https://github.com/Prithvi8706/Multiplayer-QHD-Game): co-authored manuscript, equilibrium analysis and recorded IBM Heron experiments for multiplayer quantum games.
* [FQCNN](https://github.com/Prithvi8706/Fully-Quantum-Native-QCNN): co-authored manuscript, unitary pooling and reproducible classical simulations across four classification tasks.
* [QOP](https://github.com/Prithvi8706/quantum_option_pricing): manuscript draft and reproducible encoding comparisons for Asian-basket pricing, with explicit logical-resource limits.
* [HF geolocation](https://github.com/Prithvi8706/Real-Time-Ionospheric-Geolocation): hybrid ionospheric models and Gaussian-process correction, evaluated with simulated emitter geometry.
