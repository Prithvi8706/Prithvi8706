**Prithvi Raghu**. I research quantum machine learning and scientific computing.

I build quantum convolutional networks, study multiplayer quantum games and compare circuit encodings for option pricing. I also model HF radio propagation and evaluate ionospheric corrections for emitter geolocation.

**Selected work**

* [Quantum games (QHD)](https://github.com/Prithvi8706/Multiplayer-QHD-Game): equilibrium analysis and recorded IBM Heron experiments for multiplayer quantum games, with a co-authored manuscript.
* [Quantum ML (FQCNN)](https://github.com/Prithvi8706/Fully-Quantum-Native-QCNN): unitary pooling with reproducible classical simulations across four classification tasks, documented in a co-authored manuscript.
* [Option pricing (QOP)](https://github.com/Prithvi8706/quantum_option_pricing): encoding comparisons for Asian-basket pricing, with reproducible logical-resource evidence and a manuscript draft.
* [HF geolocation](https://github.com/Prithvi8706/Real-Time-Ionospheric-Geolocation): hybrid ionospheric models and Gaussian-process correction, evaluated with simulated emitter geometry.
